CURATED ROADMAP

From fundamentals to production judgement

  1. Foundations: Linux and networking basics, processes and permissions, containers, Kubernetes objects, Git, and cloud primitives.
  2. Practitioner skills: workload rollouts, resource requests and limits, service discovery, Terraform state, IAM, and idempotent configuration.
  3. Operational depth: systemd and memory troubleshooting, DNS/TLS, metrics and traces, SLOs, noisy alerts, and safe deployment recovery.
  4. Senior-level reasoning: incident command, error-budget trade-offs, capacity, blast radius, failure domains, and reliability design.
